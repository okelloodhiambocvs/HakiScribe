Court is now in session. We shall hear Criminal Case forty five. The accused, Peter Mwangi, faces one count of burglary in Nakuru. I locked the shop at nine in the evening and went straight home. Tell the court what you found the following morning. The back window was broken and two radios were missing from the shelf. My client denies the charge and asks for reasonable bail terms. Bail is set at fifty thousand shillings with one surety of the same amount. The matter is mentioned again on the fourteenth of January.
